## 🚀 Deploy in 3 Simple Steps

### FASTEST METHOD: Netlify (Recommended)

**Total Time: 5 minutes**

1. **Sign Up**
   - Go to www.netlify.com
   - Click "Sign Up" (use email)
   - Verify your email

2. **Deploy**
   - Drag the `interior-design-site` folder to Netlify
   - Wait 30 seconds
   - Your site is LIVE! 🎉

3. **Customize URL**
   - Click "Site settings"
   - Change site name to something professional
   - Get: yourname.netlify.app

**Done! Share your website with the world!**

See **DEPLOYMENT-GUIDE.md** for detailed steps with screenshots and alternatives.

## 🎨 Quick Customization (30 minutes)

### Must-Do Changes

1. **Update Contact Info** (5 min)
   - File: `index.html` line ~560
   - Change phone: `+91 XXXXX XXXXX`
   - Change email: `info@eleganceinteriors.com`
   - Change address: `Your City, India`

2. **Add Your Logo** (5 min)
   - Upload logo.png to folder
   - File: `index.html` line ~35
   - Replace text with image

3. **Update Company Name** (5 min)
   - File: `index.html`
   - Find & Replace: "Elegance Interiors" → "Your Company"

4. **Change Colors (Optional)** (5 min)
   - File: `styles.css` lines 8-13
   - Update `--primary-color: #C9A86A;` to your brand color

5. **Add Social Media Links** (5 min)
   - File: `index.html` line ~710
   - Add your Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest URLs

6. **Connect Contact Form** (5 min)
   - Sign up at formspree.io (free)
   - Update form action in `script.js`
