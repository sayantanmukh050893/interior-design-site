Our Story

From Teaching to Transforming

After years of inspiring students in the art and science of interior design, I've embarked on a journey to bring that same passion, knowledge, and dedication directly to your spaces.

Teaching interior design has given me a profound understanding of principles that many learn only through years of trial and error. Each lecture, each student project, and each design challenge has deepened my expertise in creating spaces that are not just beautiful, but functional, sustainable, and deeply personal.

Now, I'm channeling that foundation into a consultancy that blends academic rigor with real-world creativity. Whether you're renovating a single room or reimagining your entire home, I bring the same care and insight I've always brought to the classroom — with you as my most valued student.

Our Mission

To create spaces that harmonize aesthetics with functionality, guided by expertise, enriched by creativity, and tailored to you.

Our Team

Meet The Expert

Bringing passion, expertise, and personalized attention to every project

Madhuja Ghosh

Interior Designer & Design Consultant

Madhuja Ghosh is a passionate and accomplished Interior Designer with over 9+ years of experience in teaching Interior Designing and mentoring aspiring designers. With a strong foundation in design principles and practical industry knowledge, she has successfully guided students to transform creativity into structured, functional, and aesthetically refined spaces.

Her expertise spans across multiple dimensions of interior design, including residential and commercial space planning, color theory, furniture design, lighting concepts, material selection, spatial ergonomics, sustainable design practices, and design psychology. She brings a balanced approach that blends artistic vision with technical precision.

Areas of Specialization

  • Concept development and theme-based interior styling
  • Space optimization and functional planning
  • Modular kitchen and wardrobe design
  • Lighting design and ambience creation
  • Sustainable and eco-conscious interiors
  • AutoCAD, SketchUp, and 3D visualization techniques
  • Client presentation and design communication skills
